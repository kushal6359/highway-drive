# Art bible — Frostspine

**Identity:** quiet, high, cold and dramatic. The road and ridgeline should remain legible in low visibility.

- **Palette:** cool whites, deep blues, spruce green, amber window light.
- **Terrain:** foothills, switchbacks, alpine forest, bare rock, glacial tarns and snow fields.
- **Flora:** spruce and pine below the treeline, sparse alpine grass above it, then exposed rock and snow.
- **Road:** grit, snow banks, reflective studs, stone parapets, avalanche galleries and ploughed shoulders.
- **Structures:** Skarra Pass, Kettle Springs, Glass Tarn, cable-car line, observatory dome and Long Hush Tunnel.
- **Weather/light:** snowfall, cloud inversion, mountain wind, aurora on clear nights. Whiteout must retain safe road-edge cues.
- **Audio identity:** alpine wind, softened snowfall, hot-spring bubbling and muffled tunnel acoustics.
