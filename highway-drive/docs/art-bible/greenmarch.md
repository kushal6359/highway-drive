# Art bible — Greenmarch

**Identity:** lush, inhabited, varied, and gently busy. Human-scale landmarks punctuate open countryside.

- **Palette:** layered greens, slate blues, warm brick, wet-road highlights.
- **Terrain:** rolling farmland, hedgerows, river delta, lake shore, forest clearings, harbour approaches.
- **Flora:** oak, birch, reeds, hedgerow shrubs and cultivated field bands. Cluster by moisture, slope and distance from water.
- **Road:** patched asphalt, clean but worn lane markings, grassy verge, drainage channels and stone walls.
- **Structures:** Oxmoor village, Hollin Wood, Port Halvard skyline and harbour cranes, long bridge, bay tunnel, railway and Lake Sorrel / Ketterby.
- **Weather/light:** rain, valley mist, cloud breaks and warm late-afternoon sun. Wetness builds gradually.
- **Audio identity:** birds, stream and lake water, distant city/harbour hum, occasional train and church bell.
