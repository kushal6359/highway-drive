# Art bible — Ember Reach

**Identity:** broad, hot, lonely, and spacious. The horizon is the hero; avoid clutter.

- **Palette:** ochre, rust, bleached blue, pink dusk. Shadows remain soft and slightly cool.
- **Terrain:** wind-worked dunes, salt crust, red mesas, dry washes, slot-canyon cuts. Long, readable silhouettes.
- **Flora:** sparse tamarisk, thorn scrub, occasional palms only near water. Cluster vegetation around drainage lines; do not scatter uniformly.
- **Road:** sun-faded asphalt, repaired cracks, sand drift on the outer shoulder, heat shimmer beyond the road.
- **Structures:** restrained roadside signage, Dunmarrow truck stop, solar field, abandoned fuel station, Tamarisk Wells oasis.
- **Weather/light:** hard high sun, dust haze, rare cloudburst, star-heavy night sky. Heat shimmer is subtle and disabled on Low.
- **Audio identity:** dry wind and insects at night; water and birds enter only near the oasis.
